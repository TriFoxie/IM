Activities and Hobbies

Making Cookies

I love making cookies, chocolate chip, peanut butter, vanilla, any flavour of cookie I can make it. I'd much prefer to eat them but making them means I get free cookies.

Eating Cookies

As I mentioned, eating cookies is the best. It is truly the best thing you could do. I always tell everyone that cookies are delicious. The most tasty. Very very yummy.

Cycling

I have a bike made out of cookies. Its hard not to eat it but riding it is very fun. Somehow it doesn't fall apart, which is very surprising due to the lackluster structural integrity of cookies, however it is a very sturdy bike nonetheless.
